Data Management

Import decks from a file, or download a copy of all your decks and folders.

Import Data accepts a SparkDeck backup file (.json) or a spreadsheet saved as CSV (.csv). Imported decks are added alongside your existing ones.

How to set up a CSV spreadsheet for import
  • The first row names the columns. Front and Back are required. You can also use Question and Answer, or Term and Definition.
  • Optional columns: Deck (cards with the same deck name go into one deck), Category, Folder (use a slash for folders inside folders, like Science / Biology), and Link (a video or web link).
  • Each row after the first is one card. Rows without both a front and a back are skipped, and you're told which rows.
  • If there is no Deck column, the cards go into a deck named after the file.
  • A file downloaded with Download Spreadsheet (CSV) is already in this format, so you can edit it in Excel or Google Sheets and import it again. It comes back as new decks, and decks whose name is already used get "(imported)" added.

Export all data

  • Backup file: the best choice for keeping a copy or moving to another device. You can load it back with Import Data.
  • Spreadsheet (CSV): opens in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets, with one card per row.
  • Text file: a plain, readable list of every deck and card, handy for printing or reading.

Help

SparkDeck helps you make flashcard decks and study them. This guide walks through each tab, then lists the keyboard shortcuts. Press Escape or the Close button to return to the app.

The four tabs

The row of tabs near the top of the page switches between the main areas of the app:

  • My Decks: find, open, and organize your decks and folders.
  • Create Deck: build a new deck from your notes or one card at a time.
  • Stats: see how much you have studied. You can hide this tab in Settings.
  • Settings: change how the app looks and sounds, and back up your data.

My Decks

Finding a deck

  • Search: type part of a deck's name or category in the search box. The list updates as you type, and a line above the list tells you how many decks match.
  • Category: pick a category from the dropdown to show only decks in that category. You can combine it with a search.
  • Clear: appears once a filter is set. It removes the search text and category so every deck shows again.

Folders

  • Add Folder: creates a folder in the place you are currently viewing, so folders can sit inside other folders.
  • Open a folder to see what is inside it. The path at the top shows where you are; choose any part of it to go back up.
  • A folder's own menu lets you rename or delete it. Deleting a folder moves its decks back to the top level. They are not deleted.

Deck buttons

  • Study: flip through the cards at your own pace.
  • Quiz: test yourself. A deck needs at least 4 cards before you can quiz on it, because multiple choice questions use the other cards as wrong answers.

The deck actions menu

Each deck has a button next to its name that screen readers announce as "Actions for" followed by the deck name. On screen it looks like three vertical dots. It opens a menu with these choices:

  • Edit Cards: add, change, or remove cards in the deck.
  • Export: download just this deck as a backup file, a spreadsheet (CSV), or a plain text file.
  • Move to Folder: put the deck in a different folder.
  • Enable or Disable Shuffle: choose whether cards appear in random order or in the order you wrote them.
  • Delete: remove the deck. You will be asked to confirm first.

Create Deck

  1. Type a deck name, pick a category (or choose Custom to type your own), and choose a folder if you like.
  2. Choose how to add cards: Auto-Generate from Notes or Create Manually.
  3. Your cards appear in a preview list, where you can edit or remove any of them.
  4. Select Save Deck. If you try to leave with unsaved cards, SparkDeck asks before throwing them away.

Auto-generate from notes

Paste your notes, then select Generate Cards from Notes. A message under the button tells you how many cards were made, or what went wrong. SparkDeck understands these layouts, one per line:

  • A question ending in a question mark, with the answer on the next line.
  • Term: definition on a single line (the definition needs at least 10 characters).
  • Term - definition on a single line, using a dash with spaces around it.
  • Definition: term on one line, with the explanation on the next line.

Create manually

Type the front and back of the card. You can also paste a video link, such as a YouTube link. While studying, the link appears under the card when you flip to the back, and YouTube videos can be played right there.

Studying

  • Cards are shuffled unless you turned shuffle off for that deck.
  • Use Flip to switch between the front and back, and Previous or Next to move between cards.
  • Your place in the deck is shown and announced as you go.

Quiz

  • Multiple Choice: pick the right answer from 4 options.
  • Written: type your answer. Small differences in spelling and punctuation are accepted.
  • Mixed: a random mix of both.

Stats

Choose Lifetime, Weekly, Monthly, or Daily. A sentence under the buttons sums up that period, followed by the number of decks created, study sessions, and cards studied.

Settings

  • Dark Mode: light text on a dark background.
  • Font Size: choose Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large.
  • High Contrast: black, white, and yellow colors for maximum readability. This overrides dark mode.
  • Reduced Motion: turns off animations and sliding or fading effects, for people who find on-screen motion uncomfortable.
  • Sound Effects: turn the flip, quiz, and study sounds on or off.
  • Stats Tab: show or hide the Stats tab, or reset your stats.
  • Import Data: load decks from a SparkDeck backup file, or from a spreadsheet saved as CSV. A CSV needs a first row naming the columns: Front and Back are required, and Deck, Category, Folder, and Link are optional. Each row after that is one card. Settings has a short guide with more detail.
  • Export: download all your decks as a backup file (to import later), a spreadsheet file (CSV) that opens in Excel or Google Sheets, or a plain text file that is easy to read or print. Each deck's actions menu (the "Actions for" button next to the deck name) has the same three choices for just that deck.

Your account

After you sign up, we email you a verification link. Until you open it, a reminder appears at the top of the app, and your account is device-only: your decks are saved on this device, but not backed up or shared with your other devices. You can keep studying in the meantime, resend the email, or select "I've verified my email" once you have clicked the link. Cloud backup then turns on, and the decks on this device are combined with anything already in your account, without deleting either.

Keyboard shortcuts

These work anywhere in the app, as long as you are not typing in a text field.

Navigation

  • D: go to My Decks
  • H: go to the top level of your folders
  • C or N: create a new deck
  • F: create a new folder
  • S: start studying the first deck
  • 1 to 9: study the deck with that number
  • Question mark: open this Help
  • Escape: close dialogs, end study, or end a quiz

While studying

  • Left and Right arrows: previous and next card
  • Space or Enter: flip the current card

During a quiz

  • Enter: submit a written answer
  • Escape: end the quiz early
